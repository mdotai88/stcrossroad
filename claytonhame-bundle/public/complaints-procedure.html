Complaints Procedure

Claytonhame, 76 St Cross Road, Winchester · St Cross Limited, Company No. 16080695 · In accordance with the Consumer Code for New Homes (Version 5)

We want you to be delighted with your new home, and we take every concern seriously. St Cross Limited subscribes to and complies with the Consumer Code for New Homes, which protects buyers from the date of reservation until two years after the date of legal completion. This page explains how to raise a complaint, how we will respond, and how you can escalate the matter independently if you remain unhappy.

1. How to make a complaint

You can complain to us verbally or in writing, although we encourage you to put complaints in writing so that nothing is lost or misunderstood. We will provide the same level of co-operation to a professional adviser or an agreed intermediary (for example, a family member or friend) acting on your behalf as we would to you directly.

2. What happens next

  1. Acknowledgement — within 5 working days. We will send you a written acknowledgement of your complaint within five working days of the complaint being made.
  2. Detailed response — within 20 working days. You can expect a more detailed written response within 20 working days of the complaint being made. Where applicable, this response will include one or more of the following:
    • an acceptance of the complaint and what action we are going to take to resolve the issue(s) raised;
    • an estimated timescale for the work required to resolve the issue(s) raised — the time may vary depending on, for example, the nature of the issues raised, any investigation work needed, the lead time for sourcing materials, and the preparation work needed;
    • a rejection of the complaint and details of the reason(s) why the complaint is rejected;
    • details of any further investigation work necessary to determine the outcome of our decision to either accept or reject the complaint, including timescales.
  3. Final response. A written final response will be provided as soon as possible after any further investigation has been carried out. It will set out which part(s) of your complaint we agree with and, where appropriate, which part(s) we disagree with and why.
  4. Resolution. Where remedial work is agreed, we will resolve it as soon as possible and within a timescale mutually agreed with you.

3. If you are not satisfied — escalation

If we cannot resolve your complaint together, you can escalate it as follows:

  • Structural warranty matters — Build-Zone. Issues that fall under the structural warranty, or Build-Zone's own dispute resolution service, should be referred to Build-Zone, the structural warranty provider for Claytonhame. Visit www.build-zone.com.
  • All other Code matters — the Independent Dispute Resolution Scheme. If your complaint concerns a possible breach of the Consumer Code for New Homes and it becomes a dispute, you may refer it to the Code's Independent Dispute Resolution Scheme (IDRS), operated by CEDR Limited. You may refer a dispute directly to the IDRS if:
    • you do not receive any response from us within 20 working days of your complaint being made; or
    • you cannot reach an amicable resolution with us within 56 calendar days of the complaint being made; or
    • defective, faulty or incomplete works or other issues are not resolved within the timescales agreed between you and us.

A dispute may be brought to the Independent Dispute Resolution Scheme after 56 days have passed since you first raised the complaint with us, and no later than 12 months after our final response to the complaint.

4. Your legal rights

Using our complaints procedure or the Independent Dispute Resolution Scheme does not affect your normal legal rights. If an issue is not covered by the structural warranty, the warranty body may give you details about the Code's Independent Dispute Resolution Scheme.
